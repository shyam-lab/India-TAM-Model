## India TAM Model

India is one of the fastest-growing economies globally — but most decisions about it are made with incomplete or aggregated data.

This project builds a **bottom-up, district-level market sizing engine** that enables founders, investors, and operators to:

- Quantify Total Addressable Market (TAM) at a granular level
- Segment opportunity by income, geography, and population tiers
- Run scenario analysis for product-market fit in India

Instead of “India is big,” this model answers:
→ *Where exactly is the opportunity, for whom, and at what scale?*

## Who is this for?

- Founders evaluating India entry or expansion
- Investors underwriting market size assumptions
- Product leaders designing India-first offerings
- Strategy teams building growth theses in emerging markets

## How to use this model

1. Select target customer segment (income group / district tier)
2. Define your product category
3. Estimate penetration assumptions
4. Generate TAM/SAM scenarios

Use cases:
- EdTech: Estimate paying users in Tier 2/3 cities
- FinTech: Size credit-eligible population by income bracket
- Hardware: Identify demand clusters for affordable devices

## Key Insights

- India is not one market — it is ~600+ micro-markets
- Income distribution heavily skews TAM assumptions
- Tier 2/3 cities often represent the largest *untapped* opportunity
- National-level TAM estimates are often misleading by 2–5x

## Why this matters for the future

This model connects to a broader question:

→ How do we build products for the next billion users?

It informs:
- AI model deployment in low-resource environments
- Device strategy for emerging markets
- Localization of software + services ecosystems
